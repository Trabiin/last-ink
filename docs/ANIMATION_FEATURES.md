# Character sheets for animation review

One sheet per character: what has to stay the same in every frame of every animation. Made from the five
rotation frames (south, south-east, east, north-east, north) and the portrait. Rule numbers refer to
docs/ANIMATION_RULES.md. "His/her right" means the character's own right.

## Bran (bran)
| What | Detail | Watch for |
|---|---|---|
| Head | Bald, grey beard and moustache, broad face | Hair popping onto the bald head (walk east) [5] |
| Face marks | Red scar on the forehead, over his right eye; one clouded eye (portrait) | Red smears spreading across the face (walk south) [5, 19] |
| Back of head | Plain skin with a red collar/scarf at the neck | The red collar climbing up the back of the head and reading as a face (idle and walk north) [6, 16] |
| Pauldron | Steel pauldron on his **left** shoulder | Swapping shoulders between frames [19]; it flips in the mirrored west views [20] |
| Axe | Single-bladed axe held low in his **right** hand, steel head, short wooden haft, orange tip | Haft growing into a long pole, head changing colour (idle north-east) [8]; swapping hands [17] |
| Clothes | Brown leather vest, red-orange belt, blue-grey trousers, brown boots | Belt colour flicker [5] |
| Build | Broad, heavy; walks with weight | A walk with no visible leg swing (walk south, north) [1] |

## Mother Gristle (gristle)
| What | Detail | Watch for |
|---|---|---|
| Hair | Grey hair pulled up into a bun, small **gold beads** in it: about 2 visible from the front and back, 1 from the side | Bead count going 1, 2, 3 within an animation (idle north-east) [10]; the bun changing shape [11] |
| Face | Blue eyes, hooked nose, wrinkles | Eyes changing colour or vanishing, the mouth disappearing (idle south, south-east) [5] |
| Staff | Gnarled wooden staff wrapped in herbs (green and orange), held upright in her **right** hand | Letting go of the staff (walk south-east) [8]; staff top changing [8]; swapping hands [17] |
| Bottles | **Two** glowing green bottles at her belt, seen from the front and side | Bottles popping into her hand or flashing at her feet [8, 10] |
| Back | Dark red-brown shawl with two light stitch marks at the bottom (north view) | Stitch marks appearing or vanishing [10] |
| Build | Hunched, short, slow; the steps still have to be visible | A shuffle where the legs don't show counts as not walking [1] |
